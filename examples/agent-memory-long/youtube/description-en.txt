Why Your AI Forgets: the Memory That Sounds Safest Fails

You told your AI the plan changed. It said "got it", and an hour later it booked the old plan. This video walks through the kinds of memory an AI agent has (what it learned in training, its desk, a summary, a searchable notebook, a form), how each one loses things, and what months of my own experiments say about which ones to combine. The one that sounds safest did the worst.
