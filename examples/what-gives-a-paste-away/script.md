Rewrite for a general audience of the article video *What Gives a Paste Away + Don't Just Say
"Ignore It"* (v1, 99 s; previous script in git history). Javier's decisions (2026-11-12):

- **Split in two.** This reel covers only **A2** `dont-just-say-ignore-it` (pubDate 2026-10-15):
  what happens after you tell the model to ignore a wrong paste. A1 (`what-gives-a-paste-away`,
  Valencia/Seville, 0 / 95) becomes a separate reel later.
- **Mechanism: both.** The leaks are mostly clarifying questions that list the paste ("ignore
  what?"), and sometimes promises to drop it by name (see `factcheck.md`, problem 2).

Other choices from the viewer/expert cold reads (v1 → v5): "upholstery quotes" → "sofa repair
quote" with a drawn sofa; carry on listed first in the verdict (lowest leakage); numbers as
"1 in N", ×6 kept; no p-values or decimals; the hook narrowed to "its next answer" (the leak is
an echo in that one reply, so the line never claims it lingers).
