# Voice clone — reading text (EN, ~90 s)

How to record: phone or headset mic a hand's width from your mouth, quiet room with soft
furnishings (no echo), no music or fan. Read calmly, like explaining to a colleague: the
tone of the videos. Pause naturally between paragraphs. One take is fine; if you stumble,
pause and repeat the sentence. Export as WAV or high-quality M4A.
