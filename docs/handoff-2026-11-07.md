## How these reels are made (rules learned this week)

- Rewrite the whole script for a general audience, not only the hook: an easy
  hook followed by a technical body is incoherent.
- Pick the angle first: the most surprising finding a non-specialist would retell,
  not the most particular story of the experiment.
- Every script goes through `reel-viewer` (only the script) and `reel-expert`
  (script + facts) before Javier sees it. Technical, not pedantic: narrative
  rounding is fine; flag only what changes the conclusion.
- Voice pace is a fixed standard: never slow the voice; cut or split the script.
  For a too-fast caption, split the line or add a TTS-only pause (`spoken` form).
- On screen: drawings, not words; no percentages or decimals unless essential;
  name models when the story is about them; one example, drawn on frame 0.
- Never schedule a reel before its article is live.
- Scheduling in Meta Business Suite: "Crear reel" composer (never bulk upload),
  customise FB vs IG text (FB with the article link, IG "link in bio", no GitHub
  links on IG), untick automatic subtitles, choose "Programar" before setting
  dates, verify date and time in the scheduled list. Old entries are deleted by
  Javier, not by Claude.
- To review a video, open it locally (`open out/main-en-vertical.mp4`).
